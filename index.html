University ABP Project Ideas (Aprendizaje Basado en Proyectos)

Real-world collaborative projects for university EFL students.

Project 1: University Club Fair Pitch

Final Deliverable: 3-Minute Video Pitch & Poster

Students create a new university student club based on a shared hobby (e.g., Tech Club, Hiking Society, Digital Art Workshop).

Milestones:
  • Phase 1: Survey classmates about their free time hobbies.
  • Phase 2: Design the club name, mission statement, and weekly schedule.
  • Phase 3: Present the pitch to the class using A2 frequency adverbs and preference expressions.

Project 2: Campus Life Podcast Episode

Final Deliverable: 4-Minute Audio Podcast

Students record a friendly conversational podcast discussing how university students balance academic life with creative and physical hobbies.

Milestones:
  • Phase 1: Write an interview script with target vocabulary (e.g., I'm really into...).
  • Phase 2: Record audio with sound effects or background music.
  • Phase 3: Share audio in class and give feedback on peer balance tips.
